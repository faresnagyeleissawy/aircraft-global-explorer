Aircraft Global Explorer

Controls

↑ / ↓Climb / dive
← / →Bank and turn
ShiftMore throttle (W / S: throttle up / down)
Mouse wheelZoom in and out
DragTilt and turn the view (right-click puts it back)
ClickShow the name and address of that building or spot
Double-clickFly to that spot on the map
MShow or hide the small map; click it to fly to that spot

V changes the view: behind the plane, pilot's seat, left or right window seat. R goes back to the start point. B flies to Burj Khalifa. H hides this.

On a trip the autopilot flies for you. Touch an arrow key to take over and press A to hand back. The Speed menu makes a long flight pass faster.

On a phone or tablet, the buttons at the bottom fly the plane, ☰ opens the menu, dragging a finger looks around and a double tap flies to that spot.

"Demo" plays a three-minute tour of everything; Esc stops it.

Search takes you to any place in the world. "Trip" flies from one airport or place to another. The address you are flying over is shown under the speed. Yellow planes are real flights; click one to see which.

Where do you want to fly?

Fly a trip as a passenger: type two airport codes (or places) and press Start flying.
