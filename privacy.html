Privacy policy for Aircraft Global Explorer

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Aircraft Global Explorer is a game for flying a plane or a helicopter over a 3D map of the world. It has no user accounts, no advertising and no analytics. The developer does not collect, store or sell any personal data.

What is kept on your device

The game keeps a few things in your own browser or app storage, so they are there next time. They never leave your device:

  • your settings, such as the chosen aircraft, view, map style, graphics and speed;
  • the places you save with the ★ button;
  • a Cesium ion access token, only if you type one in yourself;
  • a copy of the game's own files, so it starts faster.

You can remove all of it by clearing the site's data in your browser, or by uninstalling the app.

Services the game talks to

To show the map and answer searches, the game loads data from the services below. Like any website, they receive your device's IP address and the requests the game makes; searches send what you type, and some features send the position you are flying over. Each service handles that under its own privacy policy.

  • Cesium ion: 3D buildings, terrain and satellite photos.
  • OpenStreetMap Foundation: street map tiles and address search (Nominatim).
  • Photon by Komoot: place suggestions while you type, and the address under the aircraft.
  • OSRM: road routes between two places.
  • Esri: satellite photos when no Cesium token is used, and the small map's satellite view.
  • Google: photorealistic 3D tiles and their address search, only if you choose that option.
  • jsDelivr and cdnjs (Cloudflare): the CesiumJS and Leaflet program libraries.
  • GitHub Pages: hosting of the game itself.

Location

The game does not ask for or use your device's location. All positions in the game are places on the map that you choose or fly to.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date. Questions can be asked by opening an issue at github.com/faresnagyeleissawy/aircraft-global-explorer/issues.
