## Full description (max 4000 characters)

Fly anywhere on Earth over real 3D cities, terrain and satellite photos.

✈️ FLY A PLANE OR A HELICOPTER
Steer with the arrow keys or the on-screen buttons. The plane climbs, dives and banks; the helicopter hovers, turns on the spot and can land on the ground or on a roof.

🛫 RIDE A REAL TRIP AS A PASSENGER
Type two airport codes, for example DXB → LHR. The plane rolls down the real runway, takes off, climbs to cruising height, follows the path a real flight takes and lands on the runway at the other end. Watch from behind the plane, the pilot's seat or a window seat, and speed the trip up to 60 times.

🔍 SEARCH ANY ADDRESS
A map-style search bar finds any address or place in the world, pins it and flies you there. Save your favourite places with the ★ button.

🗺️ ALWAYS KNOW WHERE YOU ARE
A small map shows the country and province you are over, with a scale bar and your trip line. The address under you is shown as you fly, and buildings show their names.

🌍 THE WHOLE WORLD
3D buildings everywhere, 3,244 airports with their runways, satellite or street maps, a quick list of famous places from Dubai to Sydney, and coordinates for anywhere else.

⏸️ YOUR PACE
Pause at any time, speed up, zoom, tilt and turn the view with your finger.

No account, no ads, no tracking.

Map data: Cesium, OpenStreetMap contributors, Microsoft Bing, Esri. Airports: OurAirports.
